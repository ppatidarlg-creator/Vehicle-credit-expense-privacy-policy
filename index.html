Privacy Policy & Account Deletion

App Name: Vehicle Ledger: Expense & Work
Last Updated: September 19, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Vehicle Ledger: Expense & Work collects, uses, stores and protects user information. It also explains how users can permanently delete their account and associated data, or delete specific data individually, directly from the application.

1. Privacy Policy

Vehicle Ledger: Expense & Work ("we", "our", "us") is a mobile application designed to help users manage vehicle-related credit, payments, transactions and expenses.

By using the application, you agree to the collection and use of information as described in this Privacy Policy.

2. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use the application, we may collect and store the following information:

  • Name
  • Mobile phone number
  • Email address
  • Vehicle details
  • Credit and ledger transactions
  • Expense information
  • Payment-related records entered by the user
  • Account-related information
  • Advertising identifier (Advertising ID), used for showing and measuring ads
  • App usage and diagnostic information, used to operate and improve the application

3. Login and Authentication

Vehicle Ledger: Expense & Work provides account authentication through Mobile OTP and Google Sign-In.

Mobile OTP

When you choose to sign in using your mobile number, your mobile number may be processed for authentication and account security.

Google Sign-In

If you choose Google Sign-In, information provided by Google for authentication, such as your name and email address, may be associated with your application account.

4. Vehicle,Expense & Work Data

Information entered by you into the application may include vehicle details, credit records, payments, transactions, expenses and other ledger information.

This information is used to provide the core functionality of the application and to allow you to manage and view your records.

We may use collected information for the following purposes:

  • To create and maintain your account.
  • To authenticate your identity.
  • To provide Mobile OTP and Google Sign-In functionality.
  • To store and display your vehicle records.

  • To store and display expense records.
  • To maintain the functionality and security of the application.
  • To prevent misuse, fraud or unauthorized access.
  • To provide customer support when requested.

12. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights regarding your personal information, including the right to request access, correction or deletion of your information.

Users can delete individual records (such as a customer, work record, transaction or expense) directly within the application at any time, without deleting their account. Users can also permanently delete their entire account and associated data using the account deletion option provided inside the application.

13. Deleting Specific Data (Without Deleting Your Account)

In addition to full account deletion, Vehicle Credit & Expense allows users to delete specific pieces of data individually, while keeping their account active.

What Can Be Deleted Individually

Users can permanently delete any of the following items they have added, one at a time, without affecting the rest of their account:

  • Individual customers
  • Individual work records
  • Individual transactions / ledger entries
  • Individual expense entries
  • Partial deposit / payment entries
  • Drivers and vehicle/machine entries

How to Delete Specific Data

  1. Open the Vehicle Ledger: Expense & Work app.
  2. Go to the relevant section (for example, Customer, Expense, Driver or Vehicle).
  3. Open the specific item you want to remove.
  4. Select the Delete option (usually available from the item's menu or detail screen).
  5. Confirm the deletion when prompted.

Once confirmed, the selected item is permanently deleted from our systems and cannot be recovered. This action does not delete your account or any other data.

14. Account & Full Data Deletion

Vehicle Ledger: Expense & Work also provides an in-app option that allows users to permanently delete their entire account and all associated user data at once.

How to Delete Your Account

Users can delete their account directly from the Vehicle Ledger: Expense & Work application by following these steps:

  1. Open the Vehicle Ledger: Expense & Work app.
  2. Go to Settings.
  3. Select Delete Account.
  4. Follow the on-screen instructions.
  5. Confirm the account deletion.

What Data Is Deleted?

When the user confirms account deletion, the user's account and all associated data are deleted from our systems.

This includes, where applicable:

  • Account information
  • Name
  • Mobile number
  • Email address
  • Vehicle details
  • Credit and ledger transactions
  • Payment records entered by the user
  • Expense records
  • Other personal data associated with the account
Important:

Deletion of individual data, and deletion of your account, is permanent. Once data has been deleted, it cannot be recovered through the application.

15. Data Deletion and Retention

When you delete an individual item, or delete your account using the Settings → Delete Account option, the corresponding data is deleted from our systems.

We do not require users to send an email to request deletion of their data or account.

Certain information may be retained for a limited period only when required by applicable law or when reasonably necessary for security, fraud prevention, legal compliance, dispute resolution, or other legitimate purposes.
