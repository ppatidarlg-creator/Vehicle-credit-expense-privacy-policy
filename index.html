Privacy Policy & Account Deletion

App Name: Vehicle Credit & Expense
Last Updated: September 12, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Vehicle Credit & Expense collects, uses, stores and protects user information. It also explains how users can request deletion of their account and associated data.

1. Privacy Policy

Vehicle Credit & Expense ("we", "our", "us") is a mobile application designed to help users manage vehicle-related credit, payments, transactions and expenses.

By using the application, you agree to the collection and use of information as described in this Privacy Policy.

2. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use the application, we may collect and store the following information:

  • Name
  • Mobile phone number
  • Email address
  • Vehicle details
  • Credit and ledger transactions
  • Expense information
  • Payment-related records entered by the user
  • Account-related information

3. Login and Authentication

Vehicle Credit & Expense provides account authentication through Mobile OTP and Google Sign-In.

Mobile OTP

When you choose to sign in using your mobile number, your mobile number may be processed for authentication and account security.

Google Sign-In

If you choose Google Sign-In, information provided by Google for authentication, such as your name and email address, may be associated with your application account.

4. Vehicle, Credit and Expense Data

Information entered by you into the application may include vehicle details, credit records, payments, transactions, expenses and other ledger information.

This information is used to provide the core functionality of the application and to allow you to manage and view your records.

5. How We Use Your Information

We may use collected information for the following purposes:

  • To create and maintain your account.
  • To authenticate your identity.
  • To provide Mobile OTP and Google Sign-In functionality.
  • To store and display your vehicle records.
  • To store and display credit and payment transactions.
  • To store and display expense records.
  • To maintain the functionality and security of the application.
  • To prevent misuse, fraud or unauthorized access.
  • To provide customer support when requested.
  • To improve the application and its services.

6. Firebase Services

Vehicle Credit & Expense uses Google Firebase services to support authentication, data storage and application functionality.

Depending on the services enabled in the application, Firebase may process information such as account information, authentication information and application data.

Firebase services are provided by Google and are subject to Google's applicable privacy and security practices.

7. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect user information from unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure or destruction.

However, no method of electronic storage or transmission over the Internet can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

8. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information.

Information may be processed by service providers that are necessary to operate the application, such as authentication, database and hosting services.

We may also disclose information where required by applicable law, legal process or to protect the rights, safety and security of users and the application.

9. Data Retention

We retain user information for as long as necessary to provide the application's services and maintain the user's account, unless the user requests deletion or applicable law requires otherwise.

10. Children's Privacy

Vehicle Credit & Expense is not specifically directed toward children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable laws.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information through the application without appropriate consent, please contact us.

11. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights regarding your personal information, including the right to request access, correction or deletion of your information.

You can contact us using the email address provided below for privacy-related requests.

12. Account & Data Deletion

Users can request deletion of their Vehicle Credit & Expense account and associated personal data.

How to Request Account Deletion

To request deletion of your account and associated data, send an email to:

vehicle.ledger.pr@gmail.com

Please use the subject line:

Account Deletion Request - Vehicle Credit & Expense

Please send the request from the email address associated with your account, where applicable, and provide enough information for us to identify the account.

What Happens After a Deletion Request?

After receiving and verifying an account deletion request, we will process the request and delete the account and associated personal information that is required to be deleted.

This may include account information, name, mobile number, email address, vehicle information, credit/ledger transactions and expense records associated with the account, subject to applicable legal or security requirements.

Certain information may need to be retained for a limited period if required by law, for security purposes, fraud prevention, dispute resolution or other legitimate legal requirements.

13. Account Deletion from the App

If an account deletion option is available inside the application, users may use that option to initiate account deletion.

If the in-app deletion option is not available, users can request deletion by contacting us at:
